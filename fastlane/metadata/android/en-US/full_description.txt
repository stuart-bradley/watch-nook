Watchnook is a local-first TV and movie tracker for Android. Track what you're
watching and what's next, mark episodes and films watched (with bulk actions and
rewatch logging), see upcoming episodes for your shows, and keep watch
statistics — all on your device.

Built to replace TV Time, without the accounts and servers.

Why Watchnook:

* Local-first — your library lives on your phone, not on someone's server. It
  keeps working offline, and there's nothing to go down.
* No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, no social features.
* Import your history from TV Time, Trakt, IMDb, or Letterboxd — clean matches
  land automatically, ambiguous ones you confirm, and importing never wipes what
  you already have.
* Your data is yours — export it any time as portable JSON or a Letterboxd CSV,
  plus Android Auto Backup.
* Watch stats — episodes, hours, by genre and decade, streaks.

Metadata (titles, artwork, air-dates) comes from The Movie Database (TMDB).
This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or
otherwise approved by TMDB.
